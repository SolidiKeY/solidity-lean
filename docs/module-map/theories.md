# The data-structure theories

solkey's `find`/`save`/`read`/`write` are uninterpreted symbols whose meaning is
a taclet set. These modules are that theory on typed values, each taclet a
law stated under its solkey name.
The map from taclet to theorem is `docs/lean-key-rule-map.md`.

| Module | What it defines |
|---|---|
| `Theory/Typed/Obs.lean` | The typed Theory: a value is its observations (`Obs L`: word, live length, presence, at the locations `L`), `TVal T`, `TSto C` over `SLoc C` (a state variable's path or a dangling one); `find`/`save`/`delAt`/`copyTo`/`push`/`extend`/`pop`/`select` stated once over any location family (`ObsLoc`), a delete/strip/copy computed from the type (`TVal.del`, `strip`, `overlay`, one `Comp`onent at a time); the bridge from the typed storage (`SVal.obs`, `Storage.obs`, `obs_save`, `obs_write`, `obs_delete`, `obs_push`, `obs_pop`, …).  Split by topic into the pieces below (`docs/parallelism.md`), this module the umbrella. |
| `Theory/Typed/Obs/Paths.lean` | Typed paths, observations, storage locations, typed values read below a path. |
| `Theory/Typed/Obs/Values.lean` | Values and storages as their observations, a read step by step, the bridge of delete/strip/copy. |
| `Theory/Typed/Obs/Ops.lean` | Under a `select`, the storage operations, their bridge and laws. |
| `Theory/Typed/Mem.lean` | The Theory of memory, typed: solkey's memory laws on the typed heap (`Grown H`, `MAddrT T`): a write read back and its frame (`Grown.writeT_facts`), a grown heap keeps the older objects (`Heap.Keeps`, `SVal.keeps_copyStToM`, `Heap.Keeps.readAddr`, `Grown.read_then_old`, `Grown.readId_then_old`) and an allocation's freshness (`Grown.alloc_id_ge`), a fresh object's defaults and lengths (`Heap.Dflt`, `Heap.dflt_allocDefault`, `Heap.allocDefault_array_len`, `newArrT_copy_len`, the defaults' equations from `Typing/Values.lean`), copies between storage and memory (`SVal.copyStToM_field`, `MVal.copyMToSt_field`, `MVal.copyBelow_eq` by `MVal.copyAt_congr`); no erasure is read; the section "The laws by solkey's taclets" restates each under its taclet's name, a `\else` as its `Frame` (`Grown.readOnWrite`, `Grown.readOnWriteFrame`, `Grown.readOnAddMFrame`, `Heap.initMember`, `Heap.initSize`, `SVal.readFromCopyToStorage`, `Grown.readFromCopyToStorageFrame`, `MVal.findOnCopy`; Lean only, `Grown.alloc_fresh`). |
| `Theory/Typed/MemMirror.lean` | A copy into memory below its root: `SVal.Mirrors` (an object holds what the storage value held, member by member, past a heap), kept by allocations (`SVal.Mirrors.wk`) and made by `SVal.copyStToM` (`SVal.mirrors_copyStToM`); read at a member and an element (`SFields.Mirrors.get`, `SVec.Mirrors.getElem`). |
| `Theory/Typed/Leaves.lean` | The leaves of a type (`Ty.leaves`: words by members and in-range fixed indices, fuel by `rankBound`; `Ty.leafCount`; `Ty.mem_leaves` by `Path.direct`/`Path.out`; disjoint, `Ty.leaves_strip?_ne`, `Leaf.diverges`), finite shapes (`Ty.Finite`, its test `Ty.finiteShape`), and the expansions they give: in the Theory under `TVal.Shaped` (`SVal.obs_shaped`, `Storage.obs_shaped`) a delete and a copy are a word written per leaf (`Obs.delAt_expand`, `Obs.copyTo_find_expand`); in the interpreter unconditionally at a shape with a leaf (`Storage.delete_expand`, `Storage.write_expand`, `Storage.write_find_expand`); a push and a pop of a word (`Obs.push_prim`, `Obs.pop_prim`, `Storage.push_prim`, `Storage.pop_prim`). |
| `Theory/Segs.lean` | Two paths of segments that part (`Theory.diverges`) and the segment a length is read at (`Theory.lengthSeg`), what the frame rules' side conditions read; list functions on segments. |
| `Theory/Bridge/Denote.lean` | What a typed symbol denotes in the typed Theory (`Srt.TDen C`: a word an `Option`, a path an `SLoc`, a storage a `TSto`, a stored value a `TVal`; `Op0.denote` … `Op3.denote` over `Storage.obs`) and the bridge to its typed evaluation (`Srt.Bridge`, `Op0.denote_eval` … `Op3.denote_eval`: the value read as its observations, literally). The only Theory module that imports the signature directly; `Theory/Typed/Mem.lean` reads it (`MAddrT`, `Grown.writeT`) through `Calculus/StateFacts.lean`. |
| `Theory/Mutability/View.lean` | S3.1, view: what a run commits (`Globals`, `committed`); a body `within` `view` or `pure` commits its entry's storage (persistent and transient roots), ledger and balance in every outcome (`Prog.committed_view`, `Prog.committed_readOnly`, with callbacks `ExecP.committed_readOnly`); the transient layer's read-only frames keep both stores (`Transient.Program.readOnly_preserves`) and refuse writes and `nonpayable` callees (`Program.write_readOnly`, `Program.selfCall_nonpayable_readOnly`). |
| `Theory/Mutability/Pure.lean` | S3.1, pure: a state's `Outside` (storage, ledger, balance, transaction); a `pure` body's run commutes with replacing it (`Prog.run_withOutside`, by structural recursion; loops by `TLoop.run_map`), so its outcome, halt and locals are independent of it (`Prog.pure_independent`, `Prog.pure_halt_independent`, `Prog.pure_local_independent`); on the transient layer, of the outside and the transient cells (`Transient.Program.run_withOutsideCells`, `Transient.Program.pure_independent`). |
| `Theory/Mutability/Payable.lean` | S3.1, payable: every entry point refuses value to a non-payable entry whatever its body (`System.run_nonpayable`, `deployT_nonpayable`, `transact_nonpayable`, `ContractWorld.World.enter_nonpayable`, `World.callFrame_nonpayable`). |
| `Theory/Mutability/Assignable.lean` | S3.2: the semantic frame of an `assignable` clause at root granularity (`Frames`, `assignableRoots`), met by every read-only body (`Prog.frames_readOnly`, from S3.1) and by a body whose modifies set the clause names (`Prog.frames_of_storeAt`); the decidable `assignableCheck` and its soundness (`assignableCheck_sound`, every outcome `committed_frames`). |
| `Theory/Mutability/Closer.lean` | S3.3: frame facts for proofs: a modal fact about a read-only body strengthened with its frame at every successful end state (`holds_modal_readOnly`, `Spec.box_readOnly`; the transient layer's `Transient.holds_readOnly`, `Transient.holds_frame`); used by `Examples/Tactics/TransientStorage.lean` in place of per-example checks. |

## Reachable starting states (O12)

| Module | What it defines |
|---|---|
| `Theory/Reachable/Mode.lean` | The start mode (`StartMode`: `any`, `reachable M`) and `ValidStart`: `Fml.Valid` at every state, or at the transaction entry frames (`EntryState`, with the payable guard `System.refuses` and `allowCall`) of the worlds `Lifetime.Reachable M` reaches; validity from a contract invariant (`ValidStart.of_invariant`) and from a persistent-storage invariant proved by one box per entry (`PersistentFml.ofStorage`, `PersistentFml.invariant`, `ValidStart.of_persistent`). |
| `Theory/Reachable/CleanTail.lean` | The transaction reset keeps clean tails (`Storage.resetTransient_cleanTail`); clean tails are the invariant of an interface that exposes nothing (`ExposesNone`, `cleanTail_invariant`), so `ValidTail .clean` gives `ValidStart (.reachable M)` (`ValidTail.validStart_reachable`). |
| `Theory/Reachable/CallerModel.lean` | Reachability with entry calls under an execution relation (`Exec`, `RunE`, `StepE`, `ReachableE`, `InvariantE`, `ValidStartE`), the O11 stub `CallerStub` (`noCallback`, `withCallback J`), monotonicity in the relation on an interface's entries (`Exec.LeOn`, `ReachableE.mono`, `InvariantE.weaken`), `noCallback` as `Lifetime` (`reachableE_noCallback_iff`), the two readings agreeing on entries that call nobody back (`leOn_of_callsBack`), clean tails under callbacks (`KeepsCleanC`, `cleanTail_invariantE`). |
| `Theory/Progress/Outcome.lean` | Progress as an outcome classification (S4.1, lane VS3): a source result `Ends` (returned, `panics` with `panicData c`, `reverts` with other data, `outOfFuel`), `ns_iff_ends` (never `stuck` is exactly ending), `not_ends_stuck`; the core run's `CoreEnds` and `Prog.run_ends`/`Prog.runF_ends`/`Stmt.runF_ends` (progress by construction: `THalt` has no `stuck`). |
| `Theory/Progress/Tuple.lean` | Tuple declarations never get stuck (S4.2): `Val.not_tuple` (no plain value has a tuple type), `RVal.untuple_ok`, the slot conditions `SlotsOK` and `pushVals_ns`, the initializer fragment `TupleInit` (a call-fragment expression or a tuple literal of `ArgsFragment`), `argsFragment_le`, `tupleInit_ns`, the node step `declTuple_ns`. |
| `Theory/Progress/Fragment.lean` | The extensible statement fragment: `SFrag`/`IFrag` (the value fragment, tuple declarations, extension nodes with the sub-blocks they hand to the closure, and blocks/`if`/loops/`for` of them), `Blk`, the extension `StmtExt` (`none`, `or`, `Le`) and **the extension lemma shape** `StmtExt.Sound` later lanes prove for new statement constructs, quantified over every enclosing extension (`StmtExt.none_sound`, `StmtExt.Sound.or`), the induction hypothesis `StmtNSAtX` (`toBase`, `restrict`), monotonicity `SFrag.mono`/`IFrag.mono`, and the step lemmas `execSX_step`, `execItemsX_step`, `forIterX_step`. |
| `Theory/Progress/Calls.lean` | The joint progress induction with internal calls over the extended fragment: the program premise `CallsOKX` (`CallsOK.toX`), `FragXAt` (expression progress kept at every horizon up to `n`), `stmtNSAtX_step`, `modsX_step`, `callX_step`, `fragXAt`. |
| `Theory/Progress/Leaves.lean` | Leaf statements as sound extensions: `emitExt` (`emitExt_sound`), `revertErrorExt` (`revertErrorExt_sound`), their combination `effectExt` (`effectExt_sound`, every program), builtin statements `builtinExt` (`builtinExt_sound`, given expression progress at every horizon), all three `leafExt` (`leafExt_sound`, `leafExt_sound_callFree`). |
| `Theory/Progress/Program.lean` | The public progress statements (S4.1): `expr_progress`, `stmt_progress`, `items_progress`, `body_progress`, `call_progress` (every run of the fragment `Ends`), `callsOKX_none`, `tuple_stmt_progress` (no premise); elaborated tuple declarations `SFrag.cast_out`, `StmtD.declTuple_frag`; for elaborated programs with a P9-checked catalogue `elaborated_call_progress`, `elaborated_stmt_progress`, `elaborated_leafExt_sound`. |
| `Theory/Progress/Controls.lean` | Witnesses and negative controls for progress over `SemanticsExamples.E0`: a tuple declaration in and out of a loop (`pairDeclAt_frag`, `pairDecl_ends`, `pairDecl_run`, `pairDecl_timeout`, `loopDecl_frag`, `loopDecl_ends`, `loopDecl_timeout`), a block with a tuple declaration and `assert(false)` (`blockDecl_frag`, `blockDecl_ends`, `blockDecl_run`, `blockDecl_panics`), blocks through the extension hook (`blockExt_sound`, `nestedDecl_frag`, `nestedDecl_ends`), the elaborated `w_declTuple` (`elaborated_declTuple_frag`, `elaborated_declTuple_ends`); negative: `retPair_unframed_in_frag` with `retPair_unframed_not_ends` (the frame premise), `all_unsound` (the extension lemma is an obligation), `stuck_not_ends`; boundaries `slotsOK_refuses_reference`, `no_tuple_local`, `pairDecl_not_valFrag`. |
| `Theory/CallerModel/Static.lean` | Caller models that change nothing where a storage predicate holds (O11): `CallerModel.InertAt`, `inert_staticOnly`, `Reentry.reach_eq`, `Reentry.inertAt` (every entry guarded, the lock held); `TRun.transfer_storage`, a transfer's one run (`ExecSM.transfer_inert`), `Stmt.isTransfer`, `Prog.LockedAt`, `Prog.lockedAt_true`, `Prog.inertSafe`, `Stmt.eq_run_inert`, `ExecPM.eq_run_inert`; a box read deterministically (`holdsR_box_inert`). |
