# Assembly composition obligations

[Authoritative source](../../Solidity/Yul/Composition).

Composition depends on actual VY1 source, VY2 physical state, VY3 compiler and VY4 world certificates. Do not synthesize missing producers from interfaces, assume emitted artifact-table equality, or turn fixture oracles into kernel premises. The approved full assembly combinations remain obligations; recorded pauses on source/resource work must be respected until their owner releases them.
